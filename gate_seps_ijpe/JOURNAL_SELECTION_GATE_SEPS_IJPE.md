## Decision-rule check

- Strongest ACTUAL contribution-type precedent → SEPS (Chakraborty 2013
  in-journal; Shoaib 2023 simulation-as-contribution).
- Explicit editorial expectation unmet by present paper → IJPE's
  "essential novel scientific contribution" + "economic and financial
  consequences" (major negative, decision rule #2).
- One GO, one NO-GO → recommend the GO target.

## Recommendation

**Primary target: SEPS.** **Backup: IJPE only if SEPS desk-rejects on
scope** (not contribution), and even then the new-construct gap remains —
a fresh journal search (e.g., Health Care Management Science, OR
Spectrum, flexible-service journals) may beat IJPE on precedent.
