## Socio-Economic Planning Sciences (SEPS)

- Full title: *Socio-Economic Planning Sciences: The International Journal
  of Public Sector Decision-Making*. ISSN 0038-0121. Elsevier, hybrid.
- Publishing model: **subscription route = no publication fee to authors**;
  optional Gold OA APC **USD 3,590** (excl. taxes). → APC-free route: YES.
- JCR: **Q1** — listed in WoS categories ECONOMICS (SSCI), MANAGEMENT,
  and OPERATIONS RESEARCH & MANAGEMENT SCIENCE (SCIE); JIF **5.4**
  (2024 JCR as displayed on ScienceDirect); secondary JCR aggregators
  report 5.2 for the 2025 edition, still Q1 (rank 50/626 Economics).
  CiteScore 10.3. JIF edition values approximate — verify at submission.
- Aims/scope (official): quantitative analysis — OR/MS, statistics —
  applied to interdisciplinary problems of socio-economic planning and
  development; "strongly encourages contributions dealing with
  applications of quantitative models and techniques to important
  decision problems in the **service and public sectors**." Principal
  criterion: uniqueness/innovation of the methodology OR its application
  to an important public/service-sector problem OR the setting — at
  least one of model, application, context must be "unique and important."
- Article types: regular research articles + reviews; standard Elsevier
  EM submission. No hard length/figure limit found in public guidance.
- Data/code policy: standard Elsevier research-data policy (Data
  Statement); no SEPS-specific mandatory code deposit. Elsevier
  generative-AI disclosure policy applies.
- Turnaround (ScienceDirect): ~2 days to first desk decision, 84 days
  review, 213 days submission→acceptance (journal-reported medians).
- EiC: Rajan Batta (University at Buffalo).
- URLs: sciencedirect.com/journal/socio-economic-planning-sciences
  (+ /publish/guide-for-authors, /publish/open-access-options).

## International Journal of Production Economics (IJPE)

- ISSN 0925-5273. Elsevier, hybrid. Subscription route = **no mandatory
  APC**; optional Gold OA APC USD 4,290.
- JCR: **Q1**; JIF **10.0** (ScienceDirect); CiteScore 20.2.
- Aims/scope (official): interface between engineering and management;
  manufacturing/process industries and production in general; articles
  "need to be based on rigorous sound theory and contain an essential
  novel scientific contribution. Tracing economic and financial
  consequences in the analysis of the problem and solution reported,
  belongs to the central theme of the journal."
- Article types: regular research articles; standard Elsevier EM.
  Standard research-data policy; Elsevier AI-disclosure policy.
- URLs: sciencedirect.com/journal/international-journal-of-production-economics.

Note for SEPS: scope explicitly centers *service/public-sector* decision
problems. Outpatient clinic scheduling is a service-sector (and often
public-sector-adjacent) decision problem — a better scope anchor than
COR's computer-OR framing, but the "unique and important
application/context" criterion must be tested against published
precedents, not inferred from scope text.
