## Requirements adopted
- Article type: Article (full-length research).
- Emphasis: Operations Analytics / Management Science; papers must have
  implications for the practice of management; purely theoretical papers
  discouraged.
- Submission system: Elsevier Editorial Manager.
- Required/expected files: editable manuscript (.docx or .tex), cover letter,
  highlights (3–5 bullets, <=85 chars each), graphical abstract recommended,
  CRediT statement, declaration of competing interests, data availability
  statement, funding.
- A previously recorded ~35-page limit could not be revalidated against the
  current guide and must not be treated as a confirmed current requirement.
- Generative AI: disclose AI-tool use in a separate declaration in the
  manuscript upon submission; describe AI use in the Methods when used as
  part of the research process; AI cannot be an author; authors are
  accountable for verifying all AI output.
- Reference style: numbered square brackets in first-appearance order,
  retained at the author's explicit instruction. The current Elsevier support
  page documents this as Vancouver style, but the inaccessible Omega guide
  prevents a claim that it is currently mandatory for this journal.
