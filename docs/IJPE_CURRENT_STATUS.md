# IJPE current status (verified 2026-10-02, official Elsevier sources)

- Full title: **International Journal of Production Economics** (ISSN 0925-5273).
- Publisher/model: Elsevier, **hybrid** — standard subscription publication has **no mandatory APC**; optional Gold OA APC **USD 4,290** (excl. taxes).
- Journal Impact Factor: **10.0**; CiteScore **20.2** (shown on ScienceDirect journal page; JCR quartile Q1).
- Aims & scope (official): "topics treating the interface between engineering and management. All aspects of the subject in relation to manufacturing and process industries, as well as production in general… The ultimate objective … to disseminate knowledge for improving industrial practice and to strengthen the theoretical base necessary for supporting sound decision making." Accepted articles "need to be based on rigorous sound theory and contain an essential novel scientific contribution. Tracing economic and financial consequences in the analysis of the problem and solution reported, belongs to the central theme of the journal."
- Article types: regular research articles + invited/special issues; no separate theory/methodology track like EJOR.
- Manuscript requirements: standard Elsevier EM submission; Guide for Authors at
  https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-journal-of-production-economics/publish/guide-for-authors
- Data/code/reproducibility: standard Elsevier research-data policy — a Data Statement section is required/encouraged; no IJPE-specific mandatory code deposit (public GitHub repo URL is acceptable practice).
- AI disclosure: Elsevier generative-AI policy applies — AI-assisted writing must be disclosed; AI may not be an author.
- Figure/table limits: no hard numeric limit in the Guide for Authors; production-length norms apply.
